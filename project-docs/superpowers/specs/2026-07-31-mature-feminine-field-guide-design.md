# Mature Feminine Archetype and Shadow Field Guide Design

## Purpose

Transform the current Mature Feminine material into a coherent, thoroughly edited archetype-and-shadow field guide comparable in depth, usefulness, prose quality, and print production to the Mature Masculine book in `../maturemasculinedeck`.

The finished book is not a gender-swapped copy. It uses the strongest applicable editorial and production conventions from the masculine reference while developing a distinct feminine lifecycle model.

## Reference findings

The Mature Masculine reference establishes the quality bar through:

- Approximately 800–1,000 purposeful words for most archetype and shadow entries.
- Two attributed quotations for almost every archetype and shadow.
- Archetype declarations, a dynamic balance of two qualities, tailored teaching sections, practical embodiment, and a resonant conclusion.
- Shadow declarations, a precise account of the imbalance, the shadow's protective purpose and gifts, recognition in daily life, balancing practices, its relationship to the opposite shadow, and transformation.
- An authoritative but warm, direct, archetypal, and psychologically observant voice.
- Separate developmental, anti-fluff, line-edit, and copy-edit passes.
- A designed two-column print layout with images, profile information, opening and closing quotations, running furniture, and typography controls.
- Front matter explaining how to use the system and work with archetypes and shadows.
- Content, image, quotation, pagination, and publish-readiness checks.

The current feminine project has 21 archetypes and 42 embedded shadows. Archetype files average roughly 360 words including both shadows, while individual shadows average roughly 99 words. They have no archetype or shadow quotations and currently render an archetype and both shadows as one continuous section. The existing taxonomy also mixes lifecycle stages, functional poles, compound aliases, and deity lists.

## Scope

The book focuses exclusively on archetypes and their active/passive shadows. It does not reproduce the masculine book's separate libraries of virtue and skill chapters.

The primary structure is the recurring feminine lifecycle:

1. Maiden
2. Lover
3. Mother
4. Queen
5. Crone

These are psychological territories, not fixed ages, reproductive states, relationship statuses, biological requirements, or a claim that every woman follows one linear sequence. Women may revisit any stage repeatedly.

The final archetype count is evidence-led. No lifecycle stage has a quota. Every final archetype has exactly two shadows, and every archetype and shadow receives an exact facing two-page spread.

Artwork creation is a separate workstream. Existing suitable artwork should be reused and remapped. Every final spread still requires an image; missing or unsuitable artwork must be reported and replaced only when necessary to complete the book.

## Taxonomy design

Before prose is rewritten, the project must audit every existing archetype and shadow. Entries may be retained, renamed, moved, merged, replaced, or retired.

The audit produces a canonical map containing:

- One canonical name with no slash-separated, parenthetical, or alias-stacked title.
- Lifecycle stage.
- Distinct psychological function.
- Two complementary qualities.
- Active shadow.
- Passive shadow.
- Former entries and concepts absorbed into it.
- Mythic resonances.
- A concise reason it is distinct from its neighbors.

Alternate functional names such as Creatrix, Huntress, Oracle, Hearth Keeper, or Love Goddess may appear naturally in the prose when they clarify an expression of the archetype. Deities and culturally specific mythic figures appear only in the compact `Mythic Resonances` field beneath the image, not in the archetype title or in explanatory mythology sections.

Every archetype follows this conceptual equation:

`Mature expression = Quality A + Quality B`

Its shadows must arise from that balance:

- The active shadow inflates, weaponizes, or overexpresses one capacity without its complement.
- The passive shadow collapses, withdraws, or abdicates the complementary capacity.
- The two shadows form a recognizable psychological polarity rather than two unrelated undesirable personalities.
- Restoring the missing quality provides a credible path back to the mature archetype.

An entry survives only when it represents a necessary, nonduplicative function. Similar vocabulary alone is not duplication, but two entries that perform substantially the same psychological job must be merged or differentiated.

## Profile panel

The compact panel beneath each image contains only:

- Lifecycle stage
- Core function
- Balanced qualities
- Active shadow
- Passive shadow
- Mythic resonances
- Key qualities

Elements, directions, centers, and power animals are excluded.

Mythic resonances may draw from multiple traditions only when each association is defensible. They must not imply that distinct cultures or deities are interchangeable.

## Archetype spread

Every archetype occupies one exact two-page facing spread and contains:

- One canonical title.
- One verified opening quotation.
- Image and profile panel.
- A strong definition of the mature expression.
- Eight to twelve rhythmic declarations.
- A clear explanation of its two balancing qualities.
- An account of how each quality behaves without its complement.
- Its function in inner life, relationships, work, community, and creation where relevant.
- Distinctive gifts, responsibilities, and challenges.
- Concrete embodiment or integration practices.
- A resonant `Living as…` conclusion.
- One verified closing quotation.

Section headings may be tailored to the entry. The repeated functions must be present, but the prose must not feel generated from a rigid fill-in-the-blank template.

## Shadow spread

Every shadow occupies one exact two-page facing spread and contains:

- One canonical title and an active/passive designation.
- One verified opening quotation.
- Image and profile panel.
- A precise explanation of how the parent's balance breaks down.
- Six to nine shadow declarations.
- The psychological mechanics of the imbalance.
- Its protective purpose, wound, fear, or disowned need without diagnosing the reader.
- The useful energy hidden inside the shadow.
- Recognition in relationships, work, self-talk, and the body where appropriate.
- Practices that restore the missing balancing quality.
- Its relationship to the opposite shadow.
- Transformation back toward the mature archetype.
- A grounded concluding section.
- One verified closing quotation.

## Voice and editorial standards

The voice is:

- Authoritative but warm.
- Direct and concise.
- Archetypal and occasionally poetic when the image earns its place.
- Psychologically observant without becoming diagnostic.
- Instructive without lecturing.
- Able to hold a paradox rather than resolving it into a slogan.

Archetypes use `she/her`. Shared practices use `we/our`. Direct `you/your` is reserved mainly for reflection questions. The reader is primarily a woman exploring mature feminine development, but the book avoids rigid gender roles, biological essentialism, and universal claims about women.

The edit removes:

- Repeated ideas and examples that add no new distinction.
- Compound labels and decorative deity lists.
- Generic uplift and generic self-help advice.
- Decorative mysticism without explanatory value.
- Abstract claims where a concrete behavior would teach more.
- Inflated significance, vague transitions, throat clearing, forced rules of three, synonym cycling, and other recognizable AI-writing patterns.
- Excessive em dashes, hedging, weak qualifiers, and promotional language.

Expansion is allowed only when it adds a new distinction, application, tension, example, practice, or insight. Text may never be padded merely to fill a page.

## Quotations and research

Every final archetype and shadow has exactly two short quotations, primarily from women: writers, psychologists, poets, mystics, elders, artists, and cultural figures. An exceptional quotation from another voice is permitted when it fits more precisely than available alternatives.

Every quotation must be checked against a primary or reputable source for wording, attribution, and context. A source ledger records:

- Exact quotation.
- Author.
- Work, speech, interview, or other origin.
- Publication details where available.
- Source URL where applicable.
- Verification status.
- Assigned spread and position.

Invented, paraphrased, decontextualized, and commonly misattributed quotations are prohibited. Quotes should be short enough to work in the layout and conservative enough for quotation-rights concerns.

Mythic resonances receive the same research discipline. The source ledger records the relevant tradition and supporting source even though the printed profile lists only the names.

Like the masculine book, the reading experience remains citation-light. Research is represented through accurate synthesis, source ledgers, and a curated bibliography rather than inline academic footnotes.

## Content architecture

Canonical authoring remains in Markdown, but archetypes and shadows become independently renderable sources. The intended organization is:

```text
content/
  maiden/
    group.md
    archetypes/
    shadows/
  lover/
  mother/
  queen/
  crone/
```

Each archetype explicitly references its active and passive shadows. Each shadow explicitly references its parent, type, balancing qualities, quotes, image, profile data, and source identifiers.

Generated JSON remains derived output and is never edited by hand. The website's data loaders, routes, search data, and templates must follow the canonical Markdown. A migration ledger records every rename, merge, move, replacement, retirement, legacy slug, and image remapping. Preserve practical legacy aliases or redirects where the site architecture makes that reasonable.

## Editorial and production workflow

Perform the work in this order:

1. Inventory the current repository and preserve unrelated uncommitted work.
2. Analyze the Mature Masculine reference structure, editorial prompts, print generator, and sizing tool without modifying the sibling repository.
3. Create the full taxonomy audit and canonical map before drafting entries.
4. Migrate the content schema so archetypes and shadows are standalone sources.
5. Produce one representative archetype and both of its shadows.
6. Calibrate voice, structure, density, typography, and exact pagination on that trio.
7. Draft the remaining entries one lifecycle stage at a time.
8. Perform a whole-book developmental edit for conceptual gaps, overlap, contradiction, lifecycle placement, and shadow correctness.
9. Perform a dedicated redundancy, fluff, and AI-pattern cleanup.
10. Perform a line edit for sentence clarity, rhythm, force, paragraph logic, and declaration quality.
11. Perform a restrained copy edit for grammar, punctuation, capitalization, spelling, formatting, and style-sheet consistency. Do not reopen voice during copy editing.
12. Fit every entry to its exact spread without adding filler or deleting unique teaching.
13. Build and visually inspect the complete book.
14. Run all conceptual, editorial, research, site, print, and publish-readiness checks.

## Exact-spread production

Copy and adapt `../maturemasculinedeck/book/print-pdf/adjust_content_size.py` into this repository. Do not create a runtime dependency on the sibling repository.

Preserve the useful mechanism:

- Render one Markdown entry as a standalone PDF.
- Use PyMuPDF to measure physical page count and final-page fill.
- Target exactly two pages with a naturally filled second page.
- Iterate conservatively and record the measurements.
- Preserve front matter and unrelated content while adjusting the body.

Improve the mechanism for this project:

- Make measurement work without an Anthropic API dependency; the active Codex goal performs editorial changes.
- Provide a non-mutating measurement/report mode and a validator that returns a failing status when any entry violates the spread contract.
- Validate both standalone output and the entry's boundaries in the compiled book.
- Keep typography global. Do not shrink individual entries to tiny text.
- Start every entry on a left/even page and finish it on the facing right/odd page.
- Reject overflow, spill pages, clipping, visible padding, conspicuous underfill, and blank pages inside a spread.
- Prefer cutting redundancy when long. When short, add only missing substance identified by the editorial model.

The print generator must render archetypes and shadows as separate chapters/spreads and must keep front/back matter outside the core spread invariant.

## Quality gates

### Conceptual

- Every archetype has one unique function and exactly two complementary qualities.
- Both shadows derive visibly from the quality pair and form a coherent polarity.
- Each integration path restores the missing capacity.
- No two entries perform substantially the same psychological job.
- Lifecycle placement is defensible and nonliteral.

### Editorial

- Every section earns its place and adds a distinct idea.
- Declarations are specific, memorable, parallel where useful, and unique to the entry.
- Practices are concrete and observable.
- Terminology and pronouns follow the style sheet.
- The final copy edit is mechanical and restrained.

### Research

- Exactly two verified quotations appear on every archetype and shadow spread.
- Every quotation and mythic resonance has a complete source record.
- No fabricated quotations, false universals, or unsupported historical claims remain.

### Artwork

- Every entry has one suitable, correctly mapped image.
- Reused images match the revised concept and meet print-resolution needs.
- Missing or unsuitable images appear in an explicit report and are replaced only when necessary to complete the book.

### Print and site

- Every final archetype and shadow occupies exactly one facing two-page spread.
- No clipping, overflow, widows, orphans, broken headings, awkward quotation breaks, conspicuous underfill, or per-entry tiny type remains.
- Table of contents, stage dividers, running headers, page numbers, fonts, and image resolution are correct.
- Markdown-to-JSON synchronization, content validation, site build, book check, PDF build, and pagination validation pass.
- Rendered page images or contact sheets receive a visual inspection, not only a numeric page-count check.

The project must not claim completion while a quote, image, taxonomy relationship, build failure, or exact-spread violation remains unresolved.
