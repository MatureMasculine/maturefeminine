# Young Healer

## Qualities

- compassion
- boundaries

## Gifts

The young priestess: compassionate presence learning ethical limits.

## Growth Path

Support another's healing without taking ownership of it.

## Practices

- Ask whether help is wanted before offering it.
- Define the time, role, and limit of each act of care.

## Shadows

### Active Shadow: Rescuing Healer

#### Metadata

- slug: savior-priestess

#### Description

Compassion crosses boundaries and becomes intervention. She absorbs distress, gives beyond agreement, and measures her goodness by whether another person improves. Help then creates dependence, exhaustion, or resistance.

#### Declarations

- I cannot rest while someone is hurting.
- If I can help, I am responsible.
- Their progress proves my worth.

#### Balancing

- Ask permission and accept no.
- Return choices and consequences to their owner.
- Offer only what fits present capacity.

#### Gift

Her responsiveness becomes trustworthy care when it strengthens the other person's agency.

#### Out of Balance

- Gives unsolicited treatment
- Takes responsibility for outcomes
- Ignores fatigue and scope

### Passive Shadow: Guarded Healer

#### Metadata

- slug: wounded-healer

#### Description

Compassion retreats behind boundaries used as armor. Fear of depletion or entanglement turns careful scope into emotional absence. She can explain why a need is not hers while withholding the ordinary human warmth that requires no rescue.

#### Declarations

- Caring will pull me under.
- Distance is the only clean boundary.
- People must heal themselves.

#### Balancing

- Offer presence without promising a solution.
- Name limits warmly instead of disappearing.
- Let care be felt without taking over.

#### Gift

Her caution becomes ethical clarity when protection does not require indifference.

#### Out of Balance

- Uses policy to avoid contact
- Withdraws at visible need
- Confuses non-rescue with non-care

## Mature Expression

### Title

Mature Young Healer

### Paragraphs

The Mature Young Healer combines a permeable heart with a clear scope. She listens before acting, seeks consent, and supports capacities already present in the person she serves. She can care deeply, tolerate an outcome she cannot control, and replenish before offering more.

## Mature Declarations

- Care does not make me the owner of another's path.
- My limits keep my yes honest.
- Presence can help without fixing.
- I support agency, consent, and right-sized care.

## Balance

- Pair: Compassion ↔ Boundaries
- Shadows: Rescuing Healer ↔ Guarded Healer
- Power Animals: Doe, Dove, Seal
- Qualities: Compassionate, Boundaried, Present, Restorative, Empathic, Clear, Centered, Ethical, Grounded, Devotional

## Virtues

### Compassion

#### Description

Compassion lets another person's reality matter and moves toward suffering with warmth.

### Boundaries

#### Description
