# Young Visionary

## Qualities

- intuition
- verification

## Gifts

The young oracle: intuitive pattern-sensing ready to be tested in life.

## Growth Path

Receive impressions freely, then verify, translate, and revise them.

## Practices

- Record an intuition alongside evidence for and against it.
- Offer insight as a question or possibility, not a verdict.

## Shadows

### Active Shadow: Inflated Oracle

#### Metadata

- slug: fanatic-prophet

#### Description

Intuition escapes verification and becomes self-importance. She treats emotion, coincidence, and desire as privileged revelation, then pressures others to organize their choices around her certainty. Mystery is used to close inquiry rather than deepen it.

#### Declarations

- My knowing does not need to be questioned.
- Doubt means you are not ready for the truth.
- Every sign confirms my path.

#### Balancing

- State the observation separately from its meaning.
- Invite disconfirming evidence from trusted people.
- Let predictions be specific enough to learn from.

#### Gift

Her confidence in subtle perception becomes clear guidance when it remains corrigible.

#### Out of Balance

- Speaks uncertain futures as facts
- Claims special access to truth
- Reframes disagreement as spiritual failure

### Passive Shadow: Self-Doubting Seer

#### Metadata

- slug: lost-dreamer

#### Description

Intuition is dismissed before verification can even begin. She notices patterns and bodily signals, then explains them away to avoid being wrong or visible. Skepticism no longer tests the gift; it prevents the gift from entering conversation.

#### Declarations

- It is probably nothing.
- I should not trust what I cannot prove yet.
- Someone else will see more clearly.

#### Balancing

- Name the impression without claiming certainty.
- Track intuitive hits and misses over time.
- Act on low-stakes signals to build calibration.

#### Gift

Her humility becomes honest epistemic care when it does not require self-silencing.

#### Out of Balance

- Edits insight out of speech
- Outsources inner authority
- Waits for impossible proof

## Mature Expression

### Title

Mature Young Visionary

### Paragraphs

The Mature Young Visionary holds intuition as a living hypothesis. She listens to dreams, cycles, symbols, and the body, then asks what reality confirms. She offers what she sees in usable language and welcomes correction, allowing genuine insight to become more precise through relationship.

## Mature Declarations

- I honor intuition without making it infallible.
- I can be insightful and mistaken.
- I translate symbols into grounded choices.
- My gift helps others hear their own knowing.

## Balance

- Pair: Intuition ↔ Verification
- Shadows: Inflated Oracle ↔ Self-Doubting Seer
- Power Animals: Owl, Deer, Whale
- Qualities: Visionary, Intuitive, Humble, Attuned, Imaginative, Grounded, Clear, Curious, Wise, Purposeful

## Virtues

### Intuition

#### Description

Intuition notices patterns before the analytical mind can fully explain them.

### Verification

#### Description

Verification keeps insight answerable to evidence, impact, and revision.
