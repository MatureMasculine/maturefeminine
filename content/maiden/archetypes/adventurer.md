## Definition

The Mature Adventurer can leave familiar ground without abandoning responsibility. She prepares enough, listens to changing conditions, and knows the difference between a calling and an escape. Her freedom includes the capacity to return, repair, and remain when depth asks more courage than novelty.

The tempest current: freedom, range, and intelligent risk.

## Declarations

- My freedom can keep a promise.
- I prepare for risk without worshiping safety.
- I move toward growth, not merely away from discomfort.
- I can change course without fleeing consequence.

## Balance

Daring and Prudence are mutually correcting.

Daring supplies the energy to cross a real threshold despite uncertainty.

Prudence protects people, resources, and future choices without making safety the supreme value.

## Applications

Use daring to expand life and prudence to preserve what makes expansion possible.

## Gifts, Responsibilities, and Challenges

## Practices

- Before a leap, name the value, the risk, and the exit.
- Take one bounded risk instead of manufacturing a crisis.
