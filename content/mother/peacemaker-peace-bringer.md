# Peacemaker

## Qualities

- reconciliation
- confrontation

## Gifts

The peace-bringer current: reconciliation that can survive honest conflict.

## Growth Path

Seek a peace that includes the truth, not merely the end of visible tension.

## Practices

- Name the conflict in one neutral sentence before mediating it.
- Ask whose cost is hidden by the current calm.

## Shadows

### Active Shadow: Peace Enforcer

#### Metadata

- slug: conflict-avoider

#### Description

Reconciliation becomes intrusive without confrontation. She pressures forgiveness, edits anger, and restores surface calm before harm has been named. Her mediation protects the comfort of the room more than the people carrying the injury.

#### Declarations

- We need to move past this now.
- Anger only makes things worse.
- Both sides must be equally responsible.

#### Balancing

- Let the injured person set the pace of repair.
- Name asymmetry and harm plainly.
- Tolerate unresolved tension without forcing reunion.

#### Gift

Her gift for common ground becomes restorative leadership when truth is allowed to disturb the peace.

#### Out of Balance

- Rushes forgiveness
- Neutralizes legitimate anger
- Equates calm with safety

### Passive Shadow: Resentful Bystander

#### Metadata

- slug: righteous-judge

#### Description

Reconciliation collapses as confrontation is avoided rather than used. She withdraws, keeps a private ledger, and lets conflict harden into moral distance. Silence appears peaceful while relationship decays from the absence of an honest encounter.

#### Declarations

- Speaking will only create more conflict.
- They should know what they did.
- Distance is the mature response.

#### Balancing

- Make one direct, bounded statement of impact.
- Request a specific repair rather than rehearsing blame.
- Allow a difficult answer to remain part of relationship.

#### Gift

Her sensitivity to tension becomes patient timing when it eventually serves contact.

#### Out of Balance

- Withholds grievances
- Punishes through distance
- Waits for mind-reading

## Mature Expression

### Title

Mature Peacemaker

### Paragraphs

The Mature Peacemaker knows that conflict and peace are stages of the same repair. She can slow escalation, protect the vulnerable, name what happened, and invite responsibility without predetermining reunion. The peace she serves is not compulsory closeness but restored truth, safety, and choice.

## Mature Declarations

- I do not purchase calm with silence.
- Truth can be spoken without contempt.
- Repair cannot be forced, but it can be invited.
- Peace returns choice to everyone affected.

## Balance

- Pair: Reconciliation ↔ Confrontation
- Shadows: Peace Enforcer ↔ Resentful Bystander
- Power Animals: Dove, Elephant, Lamb
- Qualities: Calm, Courageous, Honest, Restorative, Listening, Fair, Bridging, Patient, Rooted, Compassionate

## Virtues

### Reconciliation

#### Description

Reconciliation rebuilds workable relationship after truth and responsibility have had their place.

### Confrontation

#### Description

Confrontation turns toward a real conflict directly enough for change to become possible.
