# Host

## Opening Quotation

## Profile

- Lifecycle stage: Mother
- Core function: Maintain bounded hospitality, domestic continuity, and a usable sanctuary.
- Balanced qualities: Hospitality + Privacy
- Active shadow: Martyr Host
- Passive shadow: Closed Hearth
- Mythic resonances:
- Key qualities: Devoted, Warm, Steady, Reciprocal, Boundaried, Simple, Grounded, Gracious, Attentive, Reliable

## Definition

The Mature Hearth Keeper creates places where bodies settle and belonging becomes tangible. She tends rhythm, beauty, food, and rest while preserving privacy and distributing labor. Her hearth has a door: it can welcome generously, close cleanly, and reopen without guilt.

The hearth-goddess current: warmth, continuity, and reciprocal domestic care.

## Declarations

- My welcome has shape and limits.
- Care for the hearth includes care for its keeper.
- Privacy renews my capacity for hospitality.
- Belonging is made together.

## Balance

Hospitality and Privacy are mutually correcting.

Hospitality makes room for another person's body, needs, and presence.

Privacy preserves inward space, rest, and the intimate boundaries of a household.

## Applications

Make welcome without making endless availability the price of belonging.

## Gifts, Responsibilities, and Challenges

The hearth-goddess current: warmth, continuity, and reciprocal domestic care.

## Practices

- State the limits of an invitation before resentment does.
- Receive one act of care without managing how it is given.
