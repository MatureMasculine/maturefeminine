# Community Weaver

## Opening Quotation

## Profile

- Lifecycle stage: Mother
- Core function: Create reciprocal belonging whose ties remain truthful, consensual, and repairable.
- Balanced qualities: Belonging + Accountability
- Active shadow: Enmeshing Networker
- Passive shadow: Isolated Helper
- Mythic resonances:
- Key qualities: Collaborative, Relational, Generous, Inclusive, Reciprocal, Attentive, Harmonizing, Strategic, Resilient, Joyful

## Definition

The Mature Community Weaver builds connection sturdy enough for truth. She notices who is absent, links gifts to needs, and creates norms that protect both belonging and consequence. She does not make herself indispensable; a healthy web can redistribute load and survive her stepping back.

The community-goddess current: creating durable belonging across difference.

## Declarations

- Belonging does not require secrecy or sameness.
- I connect people without owning their bonds.
- Accountability can restore the web.
- I participate as a member, not only a helper.

## Balance

Belonging and Accountability are mutually correcting.

Belonging creates mutual recognition, participation, and a felt place in the whole.

Accountability makes relationship answerable to agreements, impact, and repair.

## Applications

Connect people without trading truth, consent, or standards for cohesion.
