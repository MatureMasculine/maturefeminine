# Mother

## Qualities

- nurture
- autonomy

## Gifts

Nurture that protects dependence while preparing it to grow beyond her.

## Growth Path

Give what sustains life, then make room for life to develop its own strength.

## Practices

- Ask whether the moment calls for comfort, help, or space.
- Let a loved one complete an age-appropriate struggle.

## Shadows

### Active Shadow: Engulfing Mother

#### Metadata

- slug: devouring-mother

#### Description

Nurture overrides autonomy. She anticipates needs, prevents difficulty, and keeps herself central to another's functioning. Protection becomes possession when growth is treated as rejection or danger.

#### Declarations

- No one will care for you as I do.
- Your struggle means I have failed.
- Being needed is how I know I matter.

#### Balancing

- Wait for a request before stepping in.
- Celebrate competence that makes you less necessary.
- Build identity and support beyond caregiving.

#### Gift

Her vigilance becomes a secure base when it protects development rather than dependence.

#### Out of Balance

- Solves before being asked
- Treats separation as disloyalty
- Undermines others' competence

### Passive Shadow: Unavailable Mother

#### Metadata

- slug: absent-mother

#### Description

Nurture collapses while autonomy becomes premature self-sufficiency. She withholds comfort, provision, or protection and calls the resulting loneliness resilience. Need is treated as weakness because responding might expose her own limits or longing.

#### Declarations

- They need to learn the hard way.
- Comfort only creates dependence.
- I cannot be responsible for every feeling.

#### Balancing

- Respond to the need that actually exists.
- Offer dependable care without demanding gratitude.
- Repair absences instead of defending them.

#### Gift

Her respect for independence becomes empowering care when it is built on reliable connection.

#### Out of Balance

- Minimizes legitimate dependency
- Withdraws during distress
- Provides freedom without support

## Mature Expression

### Title

Mature Mother

### Paragraphs

The Mature Mother offers a secure base and an open gate. She notices real dependency, gives proportionate care, and gradually transfers responsibility as capacity grows. Her love can survive being less needed because its purpose is the flourishing of the life entrusted to her.

## Mature Declarations

- I nourish growth that will not belong to me.
- Care and freedom can increase together.
- I can be needed without making need permanent.
- I protect vulnerability and welcome emerging strength.

## Balance

- Pair: Nurture ↔ Autonomy
- Shadows: Engulfing Mother ↔ Unavailable Mother
- Power Animals: Bear, Cow, Elephant
- Qualities: Nurturing, Steady, Protective, Generous, Patient, Wise, Resilient, Grounded, Empowering, Trustworthy

## Virtues

### Nurture

#### Description

Nurture supplies the attention, protection, and resources on which life depends.

### Autonomy

#### Description

Autonomy honors the other as a distinct person capable of choice, struggle, and development.
