# Community Weaver

## Qualities

- belonging
- accountability

## Gifts

The community-goddess current: creating durable belonging across difference.

## Growth Path

Connect people without trading truth, consent, or standards for cohesion.

## Practices

- Make introductions with permission and a clear purpose.
- Name one tension directly before trying to restore belonging.

## Shadows

### Active Shadow: Enmeshing Networker

#### Metadata

- slug: manipulative-networker

#### Description

Belonging outruns accountability. She brokers closeness, loyalty, and information to stay central, smoothing over harm when truth might divide the group. Connection becomes a web that is difficult to leave without social cost.

#### Declarations

- Everyone should be included on my terms.
- Naming harm would fracture the circle.
- I know who needs to be connected.

#### Balancing

- Ask consent before sharing stories or contacts.
- Let relationships exist without your mediation.
- Address harm even when cohesion becomes uncertain.

#### Gift

Her social intelligence becomes connective stewardship when people remain free inside the network.

#### Out of Balance

- Triangulates through private information
- Protects insiders from consequence
- Makes herself the relational hub

### Passive Shadow: Isolated Helper

#### Metadata

- slug: isolated-helper

#### Description

Belonging withdraws behind accountability turned self-sufficiency. She contributes tasks and standards but does not risk mutual need, using usefulness as a substitute for membership. Others can rely on her labor yet cannot know or support her.

#### Declarations

- I belong through what I provide.
- Need would compromise my independence.
- Groups always become messy.

#### Balancing

- Ask the community for one concrete form of support.
- Share experience, not only expertise.
- Remain present through a repairable disappointment.

#### Gift

Her independence becomes dependable contribution when it participates in reciprocal belonging.

#### Out of Balance

- Serves without receiving
- Leaves at ordinary conflict
- Uses critique to remain outside

## Mature Expression

### Title

Mature Community Weaver

### Paragraphs

The Mature Community Weaver builds connection sturdy enough for truth. She notices who is absent, links gifts to needs, and creates norms that protect both belonging and consequence. She does not make herself indispensable; a healthy web can redistribute load and survive her stepping back.

## Mature Declarations

- Belonging does not require secrecy or sameness.
- I connect people without owning their bonds.
- Accountability can restore the web.
- I participate as a member, not only a helper.

## Balance

- Pair: Belonging ↔ Accountability
- Shadows: Enmeshing Networker ↔ Isolated Helper
- Power Animals: Spider, Ant, Dolphin
- Qualities: Collaborative, Relational, Generous, Inclusive, Reciprocal, Attentive, Harmonizing, Strategic, Resilient, Joyful

### Accountability
