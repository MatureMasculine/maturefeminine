# Martyr Host

## Designation

Active Shadow

## Opening Quotation

## Profile

- Lifecycle stage: Mother
- Core function: Hospitality without Privacy
- Balanced qualities: Hospitality + Privacy
- Active shadow: Martyr Host
- Passive shadow: Closed Hearth
- Mythic resonances:
- Key qualities: Overfunctions and keeps score, Makes unspoken sacrifices, Treats privacy as selfishness

## Balance Breakdown

Hospitality martyrs without privacy.

## Declarations

- A good home asks nothing of its guests.
- Rest can wait until everyone is content.
- They should notice all I do without being told.

## Psychological Mechanics

Hospitality consumes privacy. She makes herself continually available, monitors everyone's comfort, and hides her needs until resentment leaks through service. The home looks welcoming but depends on one person having no protected interior life.

## Hidden Energy

Her devotion becomes sustaining warmth when welcome includes the host.

## Practices

- Name capacity before offering care.
- Let household labor be visible and shared.
- Protect time and space that are not communal.

## Opposite Shadow

Closed Hearth is the opposite imbalance of Hospitality + Privacy.

## Transformation

Integration restores Privacy and the dynamic relationship between Hospitality and Privacy. Protect host limits; reopen bounded welcome.
