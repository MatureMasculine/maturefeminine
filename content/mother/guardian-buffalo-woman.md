# Guardian

## Qualities

- protection
- trust

## Gifts

The buffalo-woman current: steadfast protection of people, thresholds, and values.

## Growth Path

Protect what is vulnerable while allowing trusted life to cross the threshold.

## Practices

- Define the threat using evidence rather than alarm.
- Name what would earn a gradual increase of trust.

## Shadows

### Active Shadow: Controlling Sentinel

#### Metadata

- slug: rigid-sentinel

#### Description

Protection expands without trust. She scans for danger, restricts others' choices, and treats uncertainty as proof of threat. Those under her care remain safe only by remaining small, dependent, or inside her perimeter.

#### Declarations

- If I relax, harm will enter.
- I know the danger better than you do.
- Freedom is not worth the risk.

#### Balancing

- Separate likely risk from imagined catastrophe.
- Share safety decisions with capable people.
- Test trust through graduated freedom.

#### Gift

Her alertness becomes wise guardianship when it protects capacity instead of replacing it.

#### Out of Balance

- Polices ordinary choices
- Moves boundaries without consent
- Cannot stand down after safety returns

### Passive Shadow: Open Gate

#### Metadata

- slug: naive-pushover

#### Description

Protection collapses into trust without discernment. She minimizes warning signs, avoids the discomfort of refusal, and leaves vulnerable people or values exposed. Calling everyone good substitutes sentiment for stewardship.

#### Declarations

- A boundary would be unkind.
- People deserve trust regardless of pattern.
- Naming danger only creates fear.

#### Balancing

- Act on repeated evidence, not promised intent.
- Make one boundary explicit and enforceable.
- Accept that protection may disappoint someone.

#### Gift

Her openness becomes resilient trust when the gate can also close.

#### Out of Balance

- Ignores repeated breaches
- Delegates safety to hope
- Avoids necessary exclusion

## Mature Expression

### Title

Mature Guardian

### Paragraphs

The Mature Guardian keeps a living threshold, not a permanent wall. She reads risk accurately, uses the least force necessary, and relaxes protection when conditions change. Her trust is neither automatic nor cynical; it is built through character, repair, and demonstrated capacity.

## Mature Declarations

- I guard what is precious without imprisoning it.
- Trust can be earned, tested, and repaired.
- A boundary is an act of stewardship.
- I stand down when protection is no longer needed.

## Balance

- Pair: Protection ↔ Trust
- Shadows: Controlling Sentinel ↔ Open Gate
- Power Animals: Buffalo, Wolf, Bear
- Qualities: Protective, Discerning, Devout, Grounded, Steadfast, Strong, Courageous, Honorable, Patient, Trustworthy

## Virtues

#### Description

Protection preserves vulnerable life, values, and thresholds from credible harm.

### Trust

#### Description
