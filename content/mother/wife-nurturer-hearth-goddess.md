# Hearth Keeper

## Qualities

- hospitality
- privacy

## Gifts

The hearth-goddess current: warmth, continuity, and reciprocal domestic care.

## Growth Path

Make welcome without making endless availability the price of belonging.

## Practices

- State the limits of an invitation before resentment does.
- Receive one act of care without managing how it is given.

## Shadows

### Active Shadow: Martyr Host

#### Metadata

- slug: martyr-homemaker

#### Description

Hospitality consumes privacy. She makes herself continually available, monitors everyone's comfort, and hides her needs until resentment leaks through service. The home looks welcoming but depends on one person having no protected interior life.

#### Declarations

- A good home asks nothing of its guests.
- Rest can wait until everyone is content.
- They should notice all I do without being told.

#### Balancing

- Name capacity before offering care.
- Let household labor be visible and shared.
- Protect time and space that are not communal.

#### Gift

Her devotion becomes sustaining warmth when welcome includes the host.

#### Out of Balance

- Overfunctions and keeps score
- Makes unspoken sacrifices
- Treats privacy as selfishness

### Passive Shadow: Closed Hearth

#### Metadata

- slug: withholding-hostess

#### Description

Hospitality goes cold as privacy becomes exclusion. She protects order, energy, or intimacy by making others feel like intruders. The hearth remains pristine but cannot perform its purpose of sheltering real, imperfect life.

#### Declarations

- Other people disturb the peace I built.
- I can connect without opening my space.
- Needs are an invasion.

#### Balancing

- Offer one clear, bounded welcome.
- Allow shared space to bear signs of use.
- Communicate a limit without withdrawing affection.

#### Gift

Her inwardness becomes restorative sanctuary when it can open and close deliberately.

#### Out of Balance

- Makes belonging conditional on tidiness
- Withholds ordinary welcome
- Uses the home to avoid relationship

## Mature Expression

### Title

Mature Hearth Keeper

### Paragraphs

The Mature Hearth Keeper creates places where bodies settle and belonging becomes tangible. She tends rhythm, beauty, food, and rest while preserving privacy and distributing labor. Her hearth has a door: it can welcome generously, close cleanly, and reopen without guilt.

## Mature Declarations

- My welcome has shape and limits.
- Care for the hearth includes care for its keeper.
- Privacy renews my capacity for hospitality.
- Belonging is made together.

## Balance

- Pair: Hospitality ↔ Privacy
- Shadows: Martyr Host ↔ Closed Hearth
- Power Animals: Cat, Hearth Hound, Wren
- Qualities: Devoted, Warm, Steady, Reciprocal, Boundaried, Simple, Grounded, Gracious, Attentive, Reliable

### Privacy
