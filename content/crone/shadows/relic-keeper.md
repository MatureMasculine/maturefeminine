# Relic Keeper

## Designation

Passive Shadow

## Opening Quotation

## Profile

- Lifecycle stage: Crone
- Core function: Release collapses while Remembrance distorts
- Balanced qualities: Release + Remembrance
- Active shadow: Scorched-Earth Crone
- Passive shadow: Relic Keeper
- Mythic resonances:
- Key qualities: Keeps expired commitments, Organizes identity around loss, Resists new life as betrayal

## Balance Breakdown

Release collapses while remembrance becomes preservation.

## Declarations

- Letting go would mean it did not matter.
- I must keep this exactly as it was.
- Moving on is disloyal.

## Psychological Mechanics

Release fails as remembrance hardens into preservation. She maintains dead roles, loyalties, and identities because letting them end feels like betrayal. The past is honored so completely that it cannot become nourishment for the present.

## Hidden Energy

Her fidelity becomes ancestral wisdom when memory can travel without the corpse of the old form.

## Recognition

Keeps expired commitments

Organizes identity around loss

Resists new life as betrayal

## Practices

- Distinguish memory from continued obligation.
- Choose a finite form for honoring what ended.
- Make one present-tense commitment after grieving.

## Opposite Shadow

Scorched-Earth Crone is the opposite imbalance of Release + Remembrance.

## Transformation

Integration restores Release and the dynamic relationship between Release and Remembrance. Preserve learning; permit ending.
