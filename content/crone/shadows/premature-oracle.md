## Profile

- Lifecycle stage: Crone

- Balanced qualities: Vision + Timing
- Active shadow: Premature Oracle
- Passive shadow: Withholding Seer
- Mythic resonances:
- Key qualities: Predicts instead of listening, Overloads people with distant implications, Uses mystery to claim authority

## Balance Breakdown

Vision intrudes without timing.

## Declarations

- They need to hear the whole truth now.
- Readiness is only resistance.
- My vision exempts me from ordinary context.

## Psychological Mechanics

Vision disregards timing. She delivers distant truths before trust, readiness, or context can hold them, then mistakes incomprehension for others' lack of depth. Even accurate insight becomes intrusive when it steals another person's unfolding.

## Hidden Energy
