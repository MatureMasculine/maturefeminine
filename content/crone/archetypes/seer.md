## Definition

The Mature Elder Visionary sees long patterns and honors human sequence. She does not use mystery to elevate herself or prediction to control the future. Her counsel arrives in plain language, at a usable scale, and leaves the listener freer to choose.

The elder oracle and moon-seer: long vision seasoned by cycles and consequence.

## Declarations

- I offer the portion of vision this moment can use.
- Silence and speech both answer to service.
- I see patterns without claiming the whole design.
- My counsel returns authority to the listener.

## Balance

Vision and Timing are mutually correcting.

Vision perceives long arcs, hidden relationships, and consequences beyond the immediate frame.

Timing respects readiness, sequence, consent, and the ripeness of action.

## Applications

Offer far-seeing truth at the moment and scale where it can become useful.
