# Rebel

## Opening Quotation

## Profile

- Lifecycle stage: Crone
- Core function: Refuse stale or illegitimate authority and build responsible freedom beyond it.
- Balanced qualities: Liberation + Responsibility
- Active shadow: Chaotic Rebel
- Passive shadow: Compliant Critic
- Mythic resonances:
- Key qualities: Playful, Subversive, Liberating, Ethical, Quick, Inventive, Insightful, Courageous, Light-hearted, Purposeful

## Definition

The Mature Rebel is loyal to liberation rather than to rebellion itself. She exposes stale norms, disrupts with proportion, and helps build forms that can hold the freedom she seeks. Humor keeps her from sanctimony; responsibility keeps her from making other people pay for her purity.

The revolutionary trickster: liberating disruption, wit, and refusal of stale authority.

## Declarations

- I rebel for a freedom I can help sustain.
- Disruption is a tool, not an identity.
- I answer for the consequences of my refusal.
- I can preserve what works while transforming what does not.

## Balance

Liberation and Responsibility are mutually correcting.

Liberation releases life and agency from domination, inherited limits, or dead forms.

Responsibility accepts impact, interdependence, and the work of building after refusal.

## Applications

Break what constrains life and accept responsibility for what the break makes possible.

- Name the freedom sought, not only the rule opposed.
