# Queen

## Qualities

- authority
- service

## Gifts

Sovereign stewardship: ordering shared life so people and purpose can flourish.

## Growth Path

Claim authority openly and make its use answerable to service.

## Practices

- For each decision, name who is served and who bears the cost.
- Delegate one whole responsibility with authority to match.

## Shadows

### Active Shadow: Tyrannical Queen

#### Metadata

- slug: tyrannical-empress

#### Description

Authority detaches from service. She centralizes decisions, manages dissent as disloyalty, and makes the realm dependent on her approval. Order becomes an extension of ego rather than a condition in which others can govern their own work.

#### Declarations

- Only I can hold the whole picture.
- Questioning me weakens the realm.
- Control is the price of excellence.

#### Balancing

- Publish the principle behind a decision.
- Give dissent a protected channel.
- Transfer real authority, not only tasks.

#### Gift

Her command becomes coherent leadership when power is used to cultivate other people's capacity.

#### Out of Balance

- Hoards decisions
- Rewards personal loyalty
- Confuses obedience with alignment

### Passive Shadow: Abdicating Queen

#### Metadata

- slug: abdicated-ruler

#### Description

Authority collapses into service without rule. She accommodates every claim, delays decisions, and calls self-erasure humility, leaving the group to contend with a vacuum of direction. Refusing power does not remove power; it lets it become informal and unaccountable.

#### Declarations

- A good leader should not impose.
- Consensus will eventually decide for us.
- My preference should come last.

#### Balancing

- Make the decision that belongs to your role.
- State a principled no without apology.
- Accept visibility and answer for the result.

#### Gift

Her humility becomes participatory leadership when she occupies the seat entrusted to her.

#### Out of Balance

- Defers necessary decisions
- Lets informal power dominate
- Apologizes for role authority

## Mature Expression

### Title

Mature Queen

### Paragraphs

The Mature Queen occupies authority without making herself the center of the realm. She sets direction, establishes fair limits, distributes power, and accepts responsibility for decisions that only she can make. Her service is structural: people flourish because the order she tends is clear, humane, and larger than her personality.

## Mature Declarations

- I use power to make shared purpose possible.
- My authority is visible and accountable.
- I decide, explain, and remain open to correction.
- A strong realm grows more capable leaders.

## Balance

- Pair: Authority ↔ Service
- Shadows: Tyrannical Queen ↔ Abdicating Queen
- Power Animals: Lioness, Swan, Regal Mare
- Qualities: Sovereign, Nurturing, Wise, Generous, Protective, Creative, Gracious, Confident, Harmonious, Inspiring

### Service
