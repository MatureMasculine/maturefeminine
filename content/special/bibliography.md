# Bibliography and Further Reading

The works below offer source texts and adjacent frameworks for archetypal reflection, shadow integration, care, authority, relationship, and social context. They do not form one school, and inclusion does not imply agreement with every premise. Archetypal claims should be read alongside work that questions gender essentialism and keeps culture, power, and material conditions visible.

## Archetype and Shadow

Estés, Clarissa Pinkola. *Women Who Run with the Wolves: Myths and Stories of the Wild Woman Archetype*. Ballantine Books, 1992.

Johnson, Robert A. *Owning Your Own Shadow: Understanding the Dark Side of the Psyche*. HarperSanFrancisco, 1991.

Jung, C. G. *The Archetypes and the Collective Unconscious*. Translated by R. F. C. Hull. 2nd ed. Princeton University Press, 1969.

Woodman, Marion. *Addiction to Perfection: The Still Unravished Bride*. Inner City Books, 1982.

Zweig, Connie, and Jeremiah Abrams, editors. *Meeting the Shadow: The Hidden Power of the Dark Side of Human Nature*. Jeremy P. Tarcher, 1991.

Collins, Patricia Hill. *Black Feminist Thought: Knowledge, Consciousness, and the Politics of Empowerment*. Unwin Hyman, 1990.

Cooper, Anna Julia. *A Voice from the South*. The Aldine Printing House, 1892.

hooks, bell. *All About Love: New Visions*. William Morrow, 2000.

Tronto, Joan C. *Moral Boundaries: A Political Argument for an Ethic of Care*. Routledge, 1993.
