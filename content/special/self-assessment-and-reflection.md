# Self-Assessment and Reflection

This assessment has no score. It is designed to locate a current demand, not to produce a type, rank, or diagnosis. Work with one real situation at a time. Write what can be observed, and leave room for uncertainty.

## Locate the Territory

- **Maiden:** What beginning asks for both openness and judgment? What first choice is actually mine?
- **Lover:** What or whom am I allowing to matter? How can participation remain wholehearted and chosen?
- **Mother:** What needs sustained care, protection, repair, belonging, or place? Does the care increase future capacity?
- **Queen:** What authority is present, and is it legitimate? Whom should its exercise serve?
- **Crone:** What has reached a limit or ending? What must be witnessed, released, revised, or carried forward?

More than one territory may be active. Select the function with the clearest immediate consequence.

## Check the Archetype

- What is the archetype's distinct job in this situation?
- Where is Quality A visible in behavior?
- Where is Quality B visible in behavior?
- How does each quality make the other more trustworthy?
- What resource, permission, skill, or support would make the mature function more available?
- What evidence would show that the function is serving life rather than an image of maturity?

## Check for the Active Shadow

- Is a useful capacity expanding beyond consent, scope, proportion, or review?
- What information is being excluded so the action can continue?
- Who carries the cost of urgency, certainty, care, force, belonging, or control?
- Which Quality B practice would restore a limit or correction?
- What needs repair if impact has already occurred?

## Check for the Passive Shadow

- Has Quality A collapsed, been delegated, or been postponed until impossible certainty arrives?
- How has Quality B become shelter, image, critique, or distance?
- What choice remains available within the actual constraints?
- Which Quality A practice is small enough to attempt without denying danger or capacity?
- What support would make participation safer and more realistic?

## Test Context and Safety

- Am I responding to a present condition or repeating a strategy from another setting?
- What power differences shape consent and consequence?
- Are workload, money, health, disability, culture, or institutional rules narrowing the options?
- Is contact safe enough for repair, or is distance currently accurate?
- Does this situation require professional, legal, medical, community, or emergency support?

Accurate protection should not be mislabeled as avoidance. Constraint should not be mislabeled as immaturity. Context belongs inside the assessment.

## Choose and Review One Practice

Complete these sentences:

- The pattern I can observe is …
- The missing quality appears to be …
- One bounded action I will try is …
- The people whose consent or feedback matters are …
- I will review the effect when …
- I will stop or change the practice if …

Afterward, record what changed in behavior, capacity, trust, and consequence. If nothing changed, revise the interpretation before intensifying the action.

## Reflection With Others

When using these prompts in a pair or group, each person speaks for herself. Listeners may reflect exact words, ask clarifying questions, and name observable impact. They do not assign shadows, infer hidden wounds, or pressure disclosure. Agree in advance on confidentiality, time, and the right to pass.
