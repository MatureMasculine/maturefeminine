# How to Read an Archetype or Shadow Spread

Every archetype and shadow receives its own facing two-page spread. The design keeps the image, profile, teaching, practices, and quotations in one field of attention. The repeated structure makes comparison possible, while tailored headings keep each entry faithful to its particular function.

## Title and Opening Quotation

The canonical title names one pattern. A shadow is also identified by its active or passive relationship to the parent balance. The opening quotation establishes a human tension rather than offering proof. Read it in conversation with the entry, not as a slogan or an endorsement of everything in its source.

## Artwork and Profile

The artwork is an imaginative threshold, not a portrait of what a woman should look like. Beneath it, the profile contains seven fields:

- **Lifecycle stage** locates the recurring territory.
- **Core function** states the psychological job.
- **Balanced qualities** gives the parent equation.
- **Active shadow** names the inflated direction.
- **Passive shadow** names the collapsed direction.
- **Mythic resonances** offers carefully limited figures from particular traditions or works.
- **Key qualities** provides a compact recognition vocabulary.

Mythic resonances are not equivalents across cultures, personality labels, or evidence of universality. Each points to a bounded mechanism and remains accountable to its own context.

## Definition and Declarations

The opening teaching defines the mature function or the precise breakdown of its balance. Declarations then speak from inside the pattern in first-person language. They are recognition tools. Agreement with one line does not assign an identity, and disagreement does not disprove the rest of the entry.

Read declarations slowly. Notice which line has charge and which line describes an observable choice. In a shadow spread, distinguish recognition from accusation: the relevant question is how the logic appears in our own behavior and effect.

## The Quality Mechanism

The parent spread explains how Quality A and Quality B make each other trustworthy. The active shadow shows Quality A operating without Quality B; its practices restore Quality B. The passive shadow shows Quality A collapsed while Quality B becomes shelter or distortion; its practices restore Quality A.

This mechanism is the shortest route through the book. When a section feels complex, return to the equation and ask what is present, what is missing, and what the missing quality would look like in the current setting.

## Recognition, Gifts, and Practices

Body sections trace the pattern through relevant areas such as inner life, relationship, work, community, creation, self-talk, or bodily cues. Not every domain applies to every entry. The shadow's useful energy is named so integration can redirect capacity rather than merely suppress behavior.

Practices are invitations to experiment, not commands detached from context. Choose one that respects consent, authority, safety, time, money, disability, and available support. Define what will be observed and when the result will be reviewed.

## Opposite Shadow and Return

Each shadow names its relationship to the opposite expression. This prevents a swing from being mistaken for integration. The path of return restores the missing quality and reconnects both capacities to the mature parent.

The closing section and quotation gather the teaching without declaring the work complete. A spread is best read as a loop: recognition, practice, consequence, and revision.
