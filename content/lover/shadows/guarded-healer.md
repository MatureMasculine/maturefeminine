# Guarded Healer

## Designation

Passive Shadow

## Opening Quotation

## Profile

- Lifecycle stage: Lover
- Core function: Compassion collapses while Boundaries distorts
- Balanced qualities: Compassion + Boundaries
- Active shadow: Rescuing Healer
- Passive shadow: Guarded Healer
- Mythic resonances:
- Key qualities: Uses policy to avoid contact, Withdraws at visible need, Confuses non-rescue with non-care

## Balance Breakdown

Compassion collapses while boundaries become armor.

## Declarations

- Caring will pull me under.
- Distance is the only clean boundary.
- People must heal themselves.

## Psychological Mechanics

Compassion retreats behind boundaries used as armor. Fear of depletion or entanglement turns careful scope into emotional absence. She can explain why a need is not hers while withholding the ordinary human warmth that requires no rescue.

## Hidden Energy

Her caution becomes ethical clarity when protection does not require indifference.

## Recognition

Withdraws at visible need

Confuses non-rescue with non-care

## Practices

- Offer presence without promising a solution.
- Name limits warmly instead of disappearing.
- Let care be felt without taking over.

## Opposite Shadow

Rescuing Healer is the opposite imbalance of Compassion + Boundaries.

## Transformation

Integration restores Compassion and the dynamic relationship between Compassion and Boundaries. Add scope and consent; add reachable care.

## Conclusion
