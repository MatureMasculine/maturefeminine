Lifecycle

Queen

Stewardship and public power; the task is to make authority serve life.

Clear rule, proportionate judgment, and visible leadership that enlarges others.

Justice

Apply truthful, proportionate consequence while preserving the possibility of repair.

Mature Justice = Truth + Mercy

  • Merciless Judge · active shadow
  • Permissive Witness · passive shadow

Queen

Occupy legitimate authority and order shared life in service of flourishing.

Mature Queen = Authority + Service

  • Tyrannical Queen · active shadow
  • Abdicating Queen · passive shadow

Luminary

Use visibility and radiance to reveal possibility beyond the self.

Mature Luminary = Radiance + Humility

  • Blinding Star · active shadow
  • Hidden Light · passive shadow

Magician

Exercise ethical, consequential transformation within a domain of responsibility or stewardship.

Mature Magician = Agency + Integrity

  • Manipulative Sorceress · active shadow
  • Powerless Mystic · passive shadow
