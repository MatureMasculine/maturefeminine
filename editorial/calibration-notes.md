## Calibration Result

The trio uses one relational function throughout: entering wholehearted intimacy and shared commitment while remaining a sovereign participant. Its printed canonical equation is `Mature Lover = Devotion + Sovereignty`.

- The Lover gives attachment real consequence while keeping judgment, consent, other commitments, and the right to leave.
- The Enmeshing Lover is the active shadow: Devotion expands without Sovereignty. Integration restores Sovereignty so devotion becomes voluntary.
- The Armored Lover is the passive shadow: Devotion collapses while Sovereignty becomes a refuge and distortion. Integration restores Devotion without weakening accurate protection.

This is the structural and tonal calibration pattern for later trios. A parent names the balanced function, each shadow shows a causal failure of one side of the same equation, and practices restore the specifically missing quality.

## Pass 1: Developmental Edit

### Decisions

- Kept Lover distinct from Creator and Healer. Lover organizes life around chosen intimacy and shared commitment; Creator brings form into being; Healer restores capacity after injury or disruption.
- Absorbed the viable Beloved and Devoted Consort material as expressions of the Lover instead of treating them as separate functions. Receiving admiration without rank, chosen surrender, loyalty to shared purpose, and dissent within commitment now appear as capacities inside the parent spread.
- Defined Devotion behaviorally as sustained attention, remembered promises, mutual influence, and action organized around what is cherished.
- Defined Sovereignty behaviorally as agency, dignity, renewable consent, independent judgment, other sources of belonging, and the ability to revise or leave.
- Made the active-shadow chain observable: uncertainty about belonging leads to over-attunement, unrequested sacrifice, indirect bids for reassurance, invisible contracts, resentment, and pressure on another person's consent.
- Made the passive-shadow chain observable: protection from coercion or grief becomes generalized distance, then admiration replaces being known, help becomes debt, influence feels like loss of rank, and intimacy remains negotiated but emotionally unavailable.
- Preserved conditional protection language. Enmeshing strategies may once have kept contact available; armor may still be accurate in unsafe settings. Context explains the strategy without erasing its impact, and integration never prescribes access where consent or accountability is absent.
- Distributed the parent across inner life, intimate and non-intimate relationship, work, community, and creation. The shadows include bodily, relational, and workplace recognition cues rather than treating romance as their only domain.
- Required every practice to be observable and bounded: a direct request, a pause before escalation, a renewable yes, a finite act of care, a scheduled bid for contact, or a named disclosure scope and exit.
- Assigned independent mythic resonances only in the profile field: Psyche to the parent, Echo to the active shadow, and the Snow Queen to the passive shadow. Each record limits what the source can support and asserts no equivalence across traditions.

### Gate

Passed. The parent teaches one necessary relational function; both shadows derive directly from its quality pair; each integration path restores the missing quality; all required spread functions are present.
