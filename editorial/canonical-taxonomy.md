# Canonical Taxonomy

## Canonical contract

Lifecycle order and slugs are locked as:

1. Maiden (`maiden`)
2. Lover (`lover`)
3. Mother (`mother`)
4. Queen (`queen`)
5. Crone (`crone`)

The stages are recurring psychological territories, not ages, reproductive conditions, relationship statuses, biological requirements, or a universal linear path. The final count is **22 archetypes and 44 shadows: 66 canonical spreads**. Stage distribution is deliberately uneven: Maiden 3, Lover 3, Mother 6, Queen 4, and Crone 6.

Every parent and shadow below has one canonical title and slug. Canonical entry routes use `/<lifecycle>/<parent-slug>/` for parents and `/<lifecycle>/<parent-slug>/shadows/<shadow-slug>/` for shadows. Alternate functional labels belong in prose, not in titles, slugs, or parallel entries.

The invariant is:

`Mature expression = Quality A + Quality B`

The active shadow inflates or weaponizes Quality A without Quality B; active-shadow integration restores missing **Quality B**. The passive shadow collapses or abdicates Quality A while Quality B becomes a refuge, distortion, or ungrounded substitute; passive-shadow integration restores missing **Quality A**. Both integrations restore the dynamic relationship between the qualities.

## Nonduplication rules

- Visionary **tests** emerging perception; Seer **times and delivers** seasoned perception; Sage **evaluates and advises**; Magician **changes a consequential pattern within stewardship**; Queen **decides and coordinates**; Justice **adjudicates** consequence.
- Creator **receives and completes** shareable form, including expressive work; Storyteller **transmits** communal meaning; Luminary **orients** through visible presence.
- Lover integrates intimate devotion, chosen surrender, shared commitment, dignity, and sovereignty as one relational function. These are not separate archetypes.
- Mother **develops** autonomy through care; Guardian **protects** a threshold; Warrior **mobilizes** force; Host **sustains** a place; Community Weaver **builds** reciprocal ties; Peacemaker **repairs** conflict.
- Healer governs compassionate intervention within consent and scope; Witness practices active accompaniment where cure, control, or continuation is unavailable.
- Adventurer moves toward the unknown; Rebel moves against stale authority. Neither is a euphemism for impulsivity.

## Mythic-resonance boundary

The map records parent candidates, not identities, proof of universality, or decorative deity lists. A later source ledger must verify each association within its own tradition and remove any that cannot bear the claimed function. Indigenous and living religious figures require heightened cultural-context review. Drafts may not compare distinct figures as versions of one “universal goddess.” Seshat, Athena, and every other named figure in this editorial map are internal provenance or field-only research candidates, never prose aliases or body content. In print, mythic figures appear only in the **Mythic Resonances** field beneath the image.

### Shadow-level research contract

Every active and passive shadow receives its **own shadow-specific resonance assignment** and one or more independently verified research/source IDs. A shadow never inherits the parent's figures, and a parent resonance must never be presented as representing either shadow. Task 1 does not invent shadow candidates merely to fill the field. Tasks 4 and 6 must research, assign, and verify the 44 shadow-level values independently; until then, the shadow Mythic Resonances field is non-printable and blocks publish readiness.
