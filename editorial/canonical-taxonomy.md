# Canonical Taxonomy

## Canonical contract

Lifecycle order and slugs are locked as:

1. Maiden (`maiden`)
2. Lover (`lover`)
3. Mother (`mother`)
4. Queen (`queen`)
5. Crone (`crone`)

The stages are recurring psychological territories, not ages, reproductive conditions, relationship statuses, biological requirements, or a universal linear path. The count is deliberately uneven: 3, 5, 6, 4, and 6 archetypes respectively.

Every parent and shadow below has one canonical title and slug. Canonical entry routes use `/<lifecycle>/<parent-slug>/` for parents and `/<lifecycle>/<parent-slug>/shadows/<shadow-slug>/` for shadows. Alternate functional labels belong in prose, not in titles, slugs, or parallel entries.

The invariant is:

`Mature expression = Quality A + Quality B`

The active shadow inflates or weaponizes Quality A without Quality B. The passive shadow is the collapse or abdication of Quality A while Quality B becomes a refuge, distortion, or ungrounded substitute. In either case, integration restores the missing quality and the dynamic relation between both qualities.

## Nonduplication rules

Vocabulary may overlap; function may not. The following boundaries govern drafting and developmental editing:

- Visionary **perceives** a pattern; Sage **evaluates and advises**; Magician **changes** a pattern; Queen **decides and coordinates**; Justice **adjudicates** consequence.
- Muse **expresses** received inspiration; Creator **completes** new form; Storyteller **transmits** communal meaning; Luminary **orients** through visible presence.
- Mother **develops** autonomy through care; Guardian **protects** a threshold; Warrior **mobilizes** force; Host **sustains** a place; Community Weaver **builds** reciprocal ties; Peacemaker **repairs** conflict.
- Young Healer learns ethical scope where helping can overreach; Elder Healer stays human at the limit where cure and control are unavailable.
- Adventurer moves toward the unknown; Rebel moves against stale authority. Neither is a euphemism for impulsivity.

## Mythic-resonance boundary

The map records resonances, not identities, proof of universality, or decorative deity lists. A later source ledger must verify each association within its own tradition and remove any that cannot bear the claimed function. Indigenous and living religious figures require heightened cultural-context review. Drafts may not compare distinct figures as versions of one “universal goddess,” and explanatory mythology sections are out of scope.
