# Taxonomy Audit

## Decision

The canonical field guide contains **24 archetypes and 48 shadows**. The lifecycle distribution is Maiden 3, Lover 5, Mother 6, Queen 4, and Crone 6. This asymmetry follows the functions that survive the tests below; it is not a quota.

All 21 current archetypes survive as distinct functions. Their compound legacy slugs are shortened, and their shadow slugs are aligned with the canonical shadow titles. Three functions present in the last pre-Markdown canonical data are restored: Magician in Queen, and Sage and Storyteller in Crone. The latter two move from their historical Queen placement because counsel and transmission of communal memory are Crone work in this lifecycle; neither requires governing authority.

## Evidence and method

This audit compares:

- the 21 archetypes and 42 shadows loaded from `content/*/*.md` at commit `789ed4e`;
- the last prior canonical data containing Sage, Magician, and Storyteller at commit `f0a4014`;
- every parent and shadow mapping in `src/_data/images.json`;
- the balance invariant in the approved field-guide design; and
- the two-pillar shadow model in `../maturemasculinedeck/ARCHETYPE_PILLARS.md`.

“Function” means the psychological job an entry performs, not its imagery, deity associations, social role, or vocabulary. An entry survives only if the job is necessary and nonduplicative, its two qualities are mutually correcting, each shadow follows from a visible imbalance, and restoring the missing quality returns the shadow toward the parent.

The historical JSON did not store shadow slugs on Sage, Magician, or Storyteller, but the historical renderer generated them from the titles. The old pages and search data verify `arrogant-know-it-all`, `withholding-sage`, `manipulative-sorceress`, `powerless-mystic`, `exaggerating-dramatist`, and `silent-keeper`. The image-map keys independently corroborate five of the six; Manipulative Sorceress is the sole restored shadow without mapped art.

## Adversarial overlap challenge

The survival table asks whether each entry can be differentiated. This challenge asks the harder inverse: **what would be lost if the apparent pair were merged?** A lifecycle adjective, mood, image, or audience is not enough to prevent a merge. The entry must have a different action, object, breakdown, and developmental result.

## Stage-placement rationale

- **Maiden** contains first contact, first risk, and the calibration of emerging perception.
- **Lover** contains mutuality, generativity, expression, devotion, and early care-with-limits.
- **Mother** contains the sustaining functions of nurture, protection, force, repair, belonging, and place.
- **Queen** contains role authority, public judgment, visibility, and ethical transformative agency.
- **Crone** contains ending, care at the limit, long sight, responsible rupture, counsel, and transmission across generations.

These are revisitable psychological territories. “Young” and “elder” name the developmental task inside a function, not the reader's age. Mother, Lover, and Devoted Consort do not require reproduction, partnership, or any social status.
