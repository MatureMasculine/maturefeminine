# Taxonomy Audit

## Decision

The canonical field guide contains **22 archetypes and 44 shadows: 66 canonical spreads**. Lifecycle distribution is Maiden 3, Lover 3, Mother 6, Queen 4, and Crone 6. This asymmetry follows function, not quota.

Nineteen of the 21 current parents survive as canonical parents. Muse merges into Creator; Devoted Consort merges with Sacred Lover under the canonical parent Lover. Three historically retired functions return: Magician in Queen, and Sage and Storyteller in Crone. Chronology-prone titles become functional titles without merging their proven-distinct jobs: Young Visionary becomes Visionary, Young Healer becomes Healer, Elder Healer becomes Witness, and Elder Visionary becomes Seer.

## Evidence and method

This audit compares:

- the 21 archetypes and 42 shadows loaded from `content/*/*.md` at commit `789ed4e`;
- commit `4352c12`, the last revision where Sage, Magician, and Storyteller were included in the live monolithic data and generated search/pages;
- commit `f042816`, where the split Queen group omitted those three and retired them from the live collection;
- the stale monolithic records still recoverable at `f0a4014`, used only to corroborate their final historical fields, not as proof they remained live;
- every parent and shadow mapping in `src/_data/images.json`;
- the balance invariant in the approved field-guide design; and
- the two-pillar shadow model in `../maturemasculinedeck/ARCHETYPE_PILLARS.md`.

“Function” means the psychological job an entry performs, not its imagery, deity associations, social role, stage adjective, or tone. An entry survives only if the job is necessary and nonduplicative, its qualities are mutually correcting, and each shadow follows directly from one imbalance. For the active shadow, integration restores missing **Quality B**; for the passive shadow, integration restores missing **Quality A**. Both returns restore the dynamic relationship.

The historical JSON did not store shadow slugs on Sage, Magician, or Storyteller, but the historical renderer generated them from titles. The old pages and search data verify `arrogant-know-it-all`, `withholding-sage`, `manipulative-sorceress`, `powerless-mystic`, `exaggerating-dramatist`, and `silent-keeper`. Image-map keys corroborate five; Manipulative Sorceress is the sole restored shadow without mapped art.

The count is an outcome, not a presumption. Images did not confer survival: Muse and Devoted Consort have complete image families but merge; Bitter Crone and Gossiping Storyteller do not become entries; Magician survives despite missing active art because its narrowed function is necessary.

## Stage-placement rationale

- **Maiden (3)** contains first contact, first risk, and calibration of emerging perception.
- **Lover (3)** contains creation, intimate devotion, and ethical healing contact.
- **Mother (6)** contains nurture, protection, force, repair, belonging, and place.
- **Queen (4)** contains role authority, public judgment, visibility, and consequential transformation within stewardship.
- **Crone (6)** contains ending, accompaniment at the limit, long sight, responsible rupture, counsel, and transmission across generations.

These are revisitable territories. Visionary, Healer, Witness, and Seer are functional names; none implies chronological rank. Mother and Lover do not require reproduction, partnership, or social status.
